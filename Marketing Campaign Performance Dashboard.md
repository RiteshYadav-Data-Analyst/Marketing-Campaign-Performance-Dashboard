# Marketing Campaign Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project is a Power BI dashboard created to analyze the performance of different marketing campaigns.

The dashboard provides a clear view of campaign spending, revenue, and ROI. It also helps compare campaign performance across different regions and campaign types.

The main purpose of this dashboard is to understand which campaigns are performing well and where the marketing budget is being used effectively.

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Excel

## Key KPIs

The dashboard includes the following key performance indicators:

- Average ROI
- Total Revenue
- Total Spend
- Best Campaign

These KPIs provide a quick overview of the overall marketing campaign performance.

## Dashboard Features

The dashboard allows users to analyze campaign performance using different views.

### Campaign Performance

Campaigns can be compared based on:

- Spend
- Average ROI

This helps identify campaigns that are generating better returns compared to their spending.

### Regional Analysis

The dashboard shows:

- Total Spend by Region
- Total Revenue by Region

This helps understand how marketing spending and revenue vary across different regions.

### Campaign Type Analysis

The dashboard also provides a comparison of different campaign types, including:

- Digital
- Traditional

This helps in understanding the performance of different types of marketing campaigns.

## Filters

The dashboard includes interactive filters for:

- Campaign Name
- Region

These filters allow users to focus on a particular campaign or region and analyze its performance.

## Key Insights

Some of the key observations from the dashboard are:

- Influencer Marketing has the highest Average ROI of around 1.10 among the campaigns.
- TV Commercials has a comparatively lower Average ROI of around 0.45.
- Africa has the highest Total Revenue at around 8.61M.
- North America has an Average ROI of around 0.75.
- Comparing Spend, Revenue, and ROI helps identify differences in campaign performance across regions and campaign types.

## Business Objective

The dashboard can help a marketing team understand campaign performance and make better use of the available marketing budget.

By comparing spending, revenue, and ROI, the team can identify campaigns that are generating better returns and review campaigns with lower performance.
